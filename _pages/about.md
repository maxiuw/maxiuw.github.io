I am a Senior Deep Learning Researcher at [Zenseact](https://zenseact.com/), working on perception and planning for autonomous driving, where I also supervise PhD student Adam Rokah. I joined Zenseact in May 2026 and received my Ph.D. from [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/profile/maciejw) in 2026 with the thesis [*Look Ma, No Labels: From Robust Perception to Self-Supervised Representation Learning for Autonomous Driving*](https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:kth:diva-388726), supervised by [Patric Jensfelt](https://www.kth.se/profile/jensfelt) and [André Pereira](https://www.kth.se/profile/andrepe). My doctoral studies were supported by the Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program ([WASP](https://wasp-sweden.org/)). During my Ph.D. I also interned at Qualcomm and was selected as an [ACM/IEEE HRI Pioneer](https://hripioneers.org/).

My research focuses on 3D computer vision and deep learning for robot perception — including self-supervised pre-training and distillation of foundation models into LiDAR backbones, domain adaptation, end-to-end planning, and diffusion models for driving. I also build VR/AR frameworks for human-robot interaction.

Find me on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Vz7-KekAAAAJ), [GitHub](https://github.com/maxiuw), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mawoz/).
